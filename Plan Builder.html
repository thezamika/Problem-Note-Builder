Dosage / concentration — how tablet count is used

      

        
Choose how the number of tablets in the SIG affects the strength shown when Frequency is Off (when directions are hidden). Written and Latin always keep the labeled strength and show the count in the directions — this setting does not change them.


        

          
1. Total dose (× tablets) Default — scale

          
Multiplies the labeled strength by the tablet count and shows only the total. Best when you chart what the patient takes per dose.

          
Amlodipine 5 MG + Take 2 tablets daily → Amlodipine 10 MG

          
500 MG × 2 → 1000 MG · 2.5 MG × 1.5 → 3.75 MG · 10/12.5 MG ×2 → 20/25 MG

        


        

          
2. Per-tablet only (ignore count) — keep

          
Ignores the tablet count completely. Always shows the strength exactly as written on the label. Count only appears in SIG when Frequency is Written/Latin.

          
Amlodipine 5 MG + Take 2 tablets daily → Amlodipine 5 MG (Frequency Off hides the “2 tabs”)

          
Use when the pharmacy label already reflects the total, or you never want to double-count.

        


        

          
3. Show both — per-tablet + total — both

          
Keeps the labeled strength but adds the calculated total in parentheses. Most transparent for reviewers.

          
Amlodipine 5 MG + Take 2 tablets daily → Amlodipine 5 MG (2 tabs = 10 MG)

          
Metformin 500 MG + 1½ tabs → Metformin 500 MG (1.5 tabs = 750 MG)

          
Fraction examples: ½ → ½ tab, ¼ → ¼ tab, 1½ → 1.5 tabs

What about Written / Latin?

        
They are not affected by this setting. They always show the labeled strength plus the full directions:

        
Amlodipine 5 MG - Take 2 tablets by mouth daily  ↔  Amlodipine 5 MG - 2 tabs PO QD

        
Only when you switch to Frequency: Off does the setting decide whether to scale, keep, or show both.


        
Recognizes decimals, fractions (½ ¼ ¾, 1/2, 1 1/2), and words (one, two, half, one and a half, a half). Ignores “1-2 tablets” ranges — no scaling applied. Sliding-scale and respiratory inhalers are never scaled. Concentration without formulation (except ER/XR/XL) is preserved.


        
How to choose

        
    
          
  • Total dose — to match MAR / total per administration (most common).
    • 
          
  • Per-tablet only — if your source already lists the total, or you want to “disregard tablets when calculating concentration” exactly as requested.
    • 
          
  • Show both — for audits / handoffs where you want no ambiguity.
    • 
        

        
Change it anytime in Settings → Medications → Dosage / concentration. It applies instantly and is saved for next time.
